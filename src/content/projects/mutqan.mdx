## Overview

**Mutqan (مُتقَن)** is a premium mobile marketplace connecting homeowners and individuals with certified, verified trade and service professionals across Morocco.

## The Challenge

Finding trustworthy local professionals for home repairs, maintenance, and technical services in Morocco has traditionally relied on word-of-mouth with no price transparency or quality assurance:

- Building a performant native mobile experience for both Android and iOS
- Establishing trust through verified pro badges, portfolio galleries, and transparent customer reviews
- Facilitating real-time communication between clients and tradespeople with instant quote requests

## The Solution

Built with **React Native CLI**, Mutqan delivers a seamless cross-platform experience. It features location-based discovery, category filtering, an interactive quote request workflow with photo uploads, and real-time client-to-professional chat messaging.

## Key Features

- **Geolocation & Discovery** — Search verified pros by city, radius, rating, and service category.
- **Interactive Quote Requests** — Clients submit service requests with photos and description to receive direct quotes.
- **Real-Time In-App Chat** — Direct messaging between clients and professionals to coordinate visits and discuss specs.
- **Professional Dashboard** — Dedicated pro portal for tracking incoming job leads, managing portfolios, and monitoring ratings.

## Technical Highlights

- **Framework**: React Native CLI, TypeScript
- **Platforms**: Android & iOS
- **Features**: Geolocation, Real-Time Messaging, Camera & Photo Uploads, Multi-language Support
